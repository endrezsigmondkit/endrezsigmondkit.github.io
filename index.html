Email tools: systeme.io and AWeber

People starting a newsletter often look at systeme.io or AWeber. Both have free or low-cost starting points and include email plus basic automation.

Limits differ: systeme.io has a free plan with more features on paid tiers; AWeber is paid after the trial. If you only need basic sending, other free tools can work too.

  • systeme.io: free plan with email, simple page, basic automation.
  • AWeber: email marketing with automation; paid after trial.
